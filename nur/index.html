Our Learning Philosophy

  1. Learn directly from qualified Imams and scholars.
  2. Participate in physical Islamic study circles.
  3. Ask questions and receive authentic explanations.
  4. Develop Islamic manners alongside religious knowledge.
  5. Use online resources as additional learning support.

A Beautiful Reminder for Those Who Seek Knowledge

  • Qur'an and Sunnah: Our divine guidance and the Prophet's ﷺ teachings and example.
  • Authentic Ilm: Protects us from ignorance, misconceptions, and fitnah.

Warning Against Unqualified and Misleading Religious Teachers

“Allah does not take away knowledge by snatching it from the people, but He takes away knowledge by taking away the scholars...” Sahih Muslim 98

Knowledge Before Opinions

“In times of fitnah, do not follow every voice that speaks about Islam. Seek authentic knowledge, learn from qualified scholars, understand the Qur'an and Sunnah, and follow the guidance of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.” Sahih al-Bukhari 71 and 7307
